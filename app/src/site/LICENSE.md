# Map data

The building footprints, road coordinates and mapped areas in `shared/src/site/bandar-bukit-raja.json`
are derived from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and are available under
the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
See [OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

The file records the extract URL, origin, projection scale, retrieval date and original
way IDs. Coordinates use a local east/north approximation suitable for this display.
The displayed window is part of Bandar Bukit Raja Phase 1 around Gamelan and Rodat,
not an official phase boundary or a complete building inventory.

Pilot assignments, zones A–D, drainage locations, roof shapes, building heights,
rainfall, sensor records and trial records are illustrative. Google Maps is linked
for visual comparison; no Google imagery or map tiles are bundled in the application.
